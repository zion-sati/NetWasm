# NetWasm 0.10.4: WIT values without a copy

NetWasm 0.10.4 is available on NuGet.org.

This release makes calls through WIT bindings cheaper. A value that crosses a
call as a few flat values (an enum, flags, a record, a tuple, an option, a
result or a variant, or a type alias of any of these) is now lowered and lifted
directly, instead of through a temporary buffer that every call allocated,
wrote, read back and freed.

## WIT bindings

- A type alias crosses as the type it names. An alias of a primitive, such as
  `type count = u64`, is the cast the primitive is.
- Enums, flags, records, tuples, options, results and variants cross a call as
  their flat values in both directions: as parameters and results of imports
  and exports. A variant sets its discriminant and the slots its case uses,
  and the others stay zero, as the Component Model's canonical ABI specifies.
- Strings and lists still copy their contents into linear memory, which their
  flat values point at, and a call whose parameters or result do not fit in
  flat values still passes them through memory.
- Bindings that a `NetWasmWit` item generates during the build pick this up
  on the next build; regenerate bindings made with `netwasm-wit-bindgen`. The
  generated C# API is unchanged.

Time per call of a WIT import that echoes its argument, measured from a
managed loop under Wasmtime 49 at the default `Oz` (best of five runs of 2
million calls):

| Argument and result | 0.10.3 | 0.10.4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `u64` (unchanged, for reference) | 30 ns | 30 ns |
| `type count = u64` | 220 ns | 29 ns |
| an enum | 222 ns | 31 ns |
| a record of two `s32` (argument only) | 140 ns | 32 ns |
| a variant with an `f64` or a record case (argument only) | 174 ns | 57 ns |

The benchmark component is also 19% smaller (16,295 to 13,170 bytes), as
the bindings no longer carry the helpers that went through memory.

## TUnit on NetWasm

TUnit on NetWasm moves to NetWasm 0.10.4 and adds `[NotInParallel]`,
`[ParallelGroup]` and `ParallelLimiter<T>` on `netwasm0.1` (#117).
